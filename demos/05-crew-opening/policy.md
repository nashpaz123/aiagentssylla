# Refund Policy — ACME Store (internal, v3)

1. Customers may request a refund within 14 days of delivery.
2. Refunds up to 100 ILS are approved automatically by the system.
3. Refunds above 100 ILS require approval by a human support agent before any commitment is made to the customer.
4. Digital products (gift cards, e-books) are non-refundable once redeemed.
5. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 business days after approval.
6. Shipping costs are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or wrong.
